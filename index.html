THE FINDING

The same period did not affect every food equally.

Observed tomato prices rose 50% from August 2022 to August 2026. Meanwhile, official egg inflation cooled from a 17.1% peak to 5.9%. That is a decline of 11.2 percentage points.

HOW TO READ

Higher means cheaper more often

Each dot shows the share of common grocery items where the chain's median observed price is below the national median.

Mydin and Econsave tie at 89%

Both chains were below the national median for 89% of compared items. That is a share of items, not an 89% price discount.

METHOD

August 2026 · common item set only · each chain-item requires observations from at least three shops.

100% means the chain was cheaper than the national median for every compared grocery item.

THE FINDING

A top-ranked chain is not cheapest for every item.

Even the leading chains were not priced below the national median for every product. The ranking shows how often a chain was cheaper, not how much shoppers would save.

05

Takeaways

What this means for you

The same shopping trolley can produce a very different bill depending on the product, shop and location.

1

Tomato prices rose 50%

That is the observed change from August 2022 to August 2026. Other products increased less, stayed stable, or became cheaper.

2

Mydin and Econsave lead at 89%

For the common products compared, both chains priced 89% of items below the national median. Availability and individual branches may differ.

3

Kelantan faces a higher relative burden

The illustrative panel equals 4.36% of median household income there, compared with 1.65% in Selangor. This is not actual household spending.
