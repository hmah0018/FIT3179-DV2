05

Takeaways

What this means for you

The same shopping trolley can produce a very different bill depending on the product, shop and location.

1

Not everything rose equally

Some grocery products became considerably more expensive between 2022 and 2026, while others were stable or cheaper.

2

Shop choice matters

The same product can have noticeably different observed prices across premise types and supermarket chains.

3

The same bill can feel different

Household incomes vary strongly between states, changing how significant the same grocery panel cost may be.
